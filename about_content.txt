## About This Dashboard

The **Myanmar Conflict Dashboard (MCD)** is an interactive tool for exploring patterns of armed conflict, political violence, and civic unrest across Myanmar since the February 2021 military coup. It provides geographic, temporal, and actor-level analysis drawn from open-source conflict data to support researchers, journalists, humanitarian workers, and the general public.

---

## Data Source

All conflict event data is sourced from the **Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project (ACLED)**.

> Armed Conflict Location & Event Data Project (ACLED). *Conflict data for Myanmar (2021–present)*. Retrieved via the ACLED API. Available at: [acleddata.com](https://acleddata.com). © ACLED.

ACLED is a globally recognised, non-partisan crisis data project that systematically codes political violence and protest events from open-source reports including local and international media, NGO reports, and government sources. It is widely used by the United Nations, humanitarian agencies, and academic researchers.

**Coverage:** February 2021 – present · **Geography:** All 330+ townships across Myanmar's 14 States and Regions.

Our team has reviewed and recoded a subset of events using local field knowledge. In particular, events involving civilian targeting with five or more fatalities have been reclassified as **Massacres**. Displayed figures may differ from official ACLED outputs.

---

## How to Use

### Overview Page
Use the **Date Range**, **Region**, and **Conflict Type** filters, then press **Apply**. The map shows event or fatality intensity by township — darker blue means more incidents. Toggle to **Quarterly Animation** to watch how patterns shifted over time.

### Actor Analysis Page
Select any armed actor from the dropdown (default: Myanmar Military Regime). The page shows that actor's geographic footprint, offensive and defensive engagement trend, and co-actors who frequently appeared in the same events.

## Key Labels & Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| Ground-based attack | Battles, artillery shelling, and armed clashes on the ground |
| Air attack | Airstrikes by military aircraft |
| Drone attack | Attacks using unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) |
| Massacres | Events where civilians were targeted and 5+ fatalities were recorded — a dashboard-level recode of ACLED data |
| Violence against civilians | Attacks, killings, or abductions targeting non-combatants |
| Arrests | Detention of individuals by any armed actor |
| Protests | Peaceful or violent demonstrations and gatherings |
| Displacement | Events involving the forced movement of civilians |
| Offensive / Defensive | Whether an actor was the initiating (offensive) or targeted (defensive) side of a clash |
| Myanmar Military Regime | Umbrella term covering the SAC, Tatmadaw, Police Forces, and affiliated militias |
| ERO | Ethnic Resistance Organisation — armed groups with long-standing opposition to the central government |
| PDF | People's Defence Force — pro-democracy resistance forces formed after the 2021 coup |
| Associated Actor | Another actor who appeared on the same side in the same conflict event |

---

## Limitations

- ACLED records **reported** events only. Incidents in remote or information-restricted areas are likely undercounted.
- Fatality figures are estimates sourced from media reports and may not reflect actual deaths.
- The **Massacres** recode is a dashboard-level classification and is not an official ACLED category.
- Actor names follow ACLED's coding methodology, with short-form names applied by our team. Minor actors may appear under slightly different names across sources.
